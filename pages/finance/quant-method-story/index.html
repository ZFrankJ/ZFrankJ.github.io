From a Threshold to STD: Finding Patterns in Data从阀值到标准差：寻找数据规律

Intro

My Quant project began with small scripts and questions that I could express in a single comparison. A threshold was an accessible starting point: compare a closing price with a fixed absolute level. As the project grew, the question changed from where a price sat on an absolute scale to where it sat relative to a changing local context.

The first stage uses the probability density function (PDF) of closing prices, in the original price units. The second uses STD, a signed deviation measured in local standard-deviation units, to form a three-dimensional state-space view.

引言

我的量化项目起步于一些小脚本，以及可以用一次比较来表达的问题。阀值是一个容易上手的起点：把收盘价与某个固定的绝对价格比较。随着项目发展，我关注的问题也发生了变化：不再只看价格处于绝对尺度上的哪个位置，而是看它相对于不断变化的局部背景处于什么位置。

第一阶段使用原始价格单位下的收盘价概率密度函数（PDF）。第二阶段将有符号的局部偏离表示为标准差的倍数，构建三维状态空间。
