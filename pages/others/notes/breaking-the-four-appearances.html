说破四相

—— 摘录他人语句

Journal entry — Wednesday, August 13, 2025

破除四相，离执去妄。

无我相，无人相，无众生相，无寿者相。

当我们不再用“我相”画地为牢、用“人相”制造对立、用“众生相”编织偏见、用“寿者相”恐惧变迁，便能以“空明之心”如实观照万物——如镜子映照山河，不迎不拒，不执不留，在虚妄中见真实，于波动中得安宁。

Journal entry — Wednesday, August 13, 2025

See through the Four Marks; let go of attachment and illusion.

No mark of self, no mark of person, no mark of sentient beings, no mark of lifespan.

When we no longer use the “mark of self” to imprison ourselves within boundaries of our own making, the “mark of person” to set ourselves against others, the “mark of sentient beings” to weave prejudice, or the “mark of lifespan” to fear change, we can contemplate all things as they truly are with an “empty and lucid mind”—like a mirror reflecting mountains and rivers, neither welcoming nor rejecting, neither grasping nor retaining; seeing truth within illusion and finding peace within flux.
